## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- ESPSomfy RTS controllers (ESP32 + 433 MHz radio, firmware 2.4.x) found on the local network through the SSDP discovery of Gladys, plus IP addresses typed in the configuration; each controller is identified by its server id and found again after an address change.
- One device per shade: roller shutters, blinds, shutters, awnings, garage doors and gates as Gladys shutters (open / stop / close and position), draperies as curtains, slats as a second position, one-button garage doors and gates as open / close, dry contacts as switches, and a "sun sensor active" switch for the motors with a Somfy sun sensor and the awnings.
- One device per group of shades: open / stop / close and the sun sensor.
- Positions counted like Gladys (100 % = open), "flip position" and awnings handled like the Home Assistant integration of the firmware author.
- States pushed by the controller over its local socket, no polling; a local or unreachable badge per device, automatic reconnection.
- Dashboard widgets "Somfy shade" (live position, state, "My" position, sun and wind, open / My / close and sun sensor buttons) and "Somfy shades" (every shade at a glance, controllers and firmware updates).
- Scene triggers: a Somfy remote is pressed (any button, by shade), sun or wind changes, a controller is lost, a controller is back.
- Scene actions: send a Somfy command ("My", open, close, a step, sun sensor) to a shade or a group, correct the position of a shade without moving it.
- Configuration button "Search the controllers".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
